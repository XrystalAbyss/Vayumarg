1. Great-Circle Geodesic Extrapolation (Module B)

Velocity vectors are calculated using the Haversine great-circle distance between consecutive historical observations strictly prior to issue time:

d = 2R · arcsin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)cos(φ₂)sin²(Δλ/2)))
φ₂ = arcsin(sin(φ₁)cos(δ) + cos(φ₁)sin(δ)cos(θ))
λ₂ = λ₁ + atan2(sin(θ)sin(δ)cos(φ₁), cos(δ) - sin(φ₁)sin(φ₂))

2. Multi-Agency Wind Averaging Standards

The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) recognizes different sustained wind averaging conventions across national agencies:

  • IMD (RSMC New Delhi): 3-minute sustained wind speed. Official standard for NIO cyclonic storm categorization.
  • JTWC (US Navy): 1-minute sustained wind speed (Atkinson & Holliday factor ~ 0.88 conversion to 3-min).
  • WMO Consensus: 10-minute sustained wind speed.

3. ISRO INSAT Multichannel Radiometry (Module A)

Thermal infrared radiance values from INSAT-3DR (74.0°E) and INSAT-3DS (82.0°E) Imagers are calibrated using official ISRO lookup tables derived from Planck's radiation law:

T_b = (c₂ · ν) / ln(1 + (c₁ · ν³) / R)
Standardized grid: 200 × 200 pixels (800 km × 800 km bounding box centered on synoptic eye)

4. Formal Limitations & Research Scope

• Baseline trajectory assumes persistence of forward translation velocity and does not incorporate NWP atmospheric steering fields or vertical wind shear.
• Satellite brightness temperatures are subject to sensor calibration LUT clamping below 180 Kelvin.
• Deep-learning intensity models remain in active R&D and require larger verified cohorts before operational deployment.
